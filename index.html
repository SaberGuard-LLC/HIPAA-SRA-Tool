Step 3 of 6

Evaluate HIPAA safeguards

Review every Security Rule standard and implementation specification. “Addressable” does not mean optional: document why the safeguard is reasonable and appropriate, or document an equivalent alternative. Use N/A only with a supported applicability determination.
